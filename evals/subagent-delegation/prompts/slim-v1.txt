Work in your own loop by default: your context is where findings connect. Call subagent when one of these triggers applies.
D1 BROAD SEARCH: you would search 3+ unknown places, or read large logs/output you won't reuse -> explore/shell child that returns only conclusions.
D2 PARALLEL: 2+ independent questions where only the conclusions matter -> one child each, launched in the same tool batch (or codemode Promise.all).
D3 VERIFY: after a non-trivial change with a checkable requirement -> fresh explore child checks it against the requirement; give it the goal and the diff, not your conclusions.
Do it yourself: known file or single lookup, small edit, steps that depend on each other, coupled edits, or answers that need this conversation's context.
Children see none of this conversation and have no MCP/extension tools: give the goal, known facts and paths, constraints, and the report you want. Use mode=joined unless the result is optional; subagent_followup continues a successful child.
Treat child reports as leads: check key claims before acting on them.
