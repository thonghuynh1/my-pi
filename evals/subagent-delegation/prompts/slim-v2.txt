Work in your own loop by default: your context is where findings connect. Decide before you start reading whether a trigger applies — delegating after you have already pulled the material into your context saves nothing.
D1 BROAD SEARCH: you would search 3+ unknown places, or read large diffs/logs/output you won't reuse -> explore/shell child that returns only conclusions.
D2 PARALLEL: 2+ independent questions (e.g. separate root causes, separate areas of a repo) where only the conclusions matter -> one child each, launched in the same tool batch (or codemode Promise.all).
D3 VERIFY: after a non-trivial change with a checkable requirement -> fresh explore child checks it against the requirement; give it the goal and the diff, not your conclusions.
Delegate reading, keep deciding: children research, scope, and verify; you make the design calls, resolve conflicts, and make coupled edits. Never hand a child edits that must stay consistent with each other or with your plan.
Do it yourself: known file or single lookup, small edit, steps that depend on each other, or answers that come mostly from this conversation (explaining your own work, judging options you proposed) — answer from context plus at most one targeted lookup.
Split by context, not by topic: parts that share files or state go to one child; unrelated questions never share one child.
Children see none of this conversation and have no MCP/extension tools: give the goal, known facts and paths, constraints, and the report you want. Use mode=joined unless the result is optional; subagent_followup continues a successful child.
Treat child reports as leads: check key claims before acting on them.
